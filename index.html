Approach

1) Define the question

We agree scope, time period, deliverables, and what “done” looks like.

2) Gather and verify

I collect sources, log citations, and note reliability, gaps, and contradictions.

3) Deliver in layers

You get a readable summary first — then the supporting detail and references.
